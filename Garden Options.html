Design 09

The white Roman courtyard

Dining + lounge · No pool

Warm-white walls, a shallow arched fountain and stone-look paving. A timber pergola frames the dining area; a separate corner sofa makes space to stretch out. Olive planting and retained bougainvillea soften the courtyard.

Why choose it? Most suited to a warm, classical atmosphere with generous usable floor space.

Design 10

The framed-wall social courtyard

Dining + curved seating · No pool

Rectangular wall-frame detailing carries the boutique-hotel look outdoors. The ground-level curved sofa and small fountain create a relaxed gathering area, with dining beside the house under a partial pergola.

Why choose it? My strongest starting point for hosting: flexible open space, comfortable group seating and no pool taking up the garden.

Design 11

The compact private resort

Dining + lounge + small plunge pool

White walls, rich timber tones, a slim bronze pergola and layered planting. The concept includes a small plunge pool, approximately 2.6 × 1.8 m of water, with compact lounge seating and a separate dining area.

Why choose it? The most resort-like option, with the tightest circulation and the largest outdoor budget. Pool size, equipment location and access need a measured layout before committing.
