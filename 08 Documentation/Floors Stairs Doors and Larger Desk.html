1. Herringbone PVC flooring

My design preference is a matte natural-oak tone, with the same product and coordinated pattern through the dry interiors. These mockups use PVC luxury vinyl tile/plank (LVT/LVP). Herringbone uses rectangular pieces meeting at right angles; it differs from chevron, whose ends are cut diagonally. Karndean's pattern guide.

Ask the installer to compare a glue-down herringbone system with a purpose-made click system after checking subfloor flatness, moisture, existing tile adhesion and finished floor heights. Specify the exact product and approved adhesive for your sunny glazed rooms. Do not assume every PVC product can float under fixed joinery or that existing tiles need no preparation. Manufacturer installation guidance.

The new designs keep porcelain in bathrooms, powder room and wet laundry utility, and retain the existing stone stair treads. Outdoor wood-effect or herringbone paving shown is exterior-rated porcelain, not indoor PVC. Confirm threshold details at bathrooms, the stair landing and external doors. Review a physical floor sample beside the stone stairs before ordering.

2. Replace the complete stair and upstairs balustrade

Balusters are the vertical pieces; the handrail is the part you hold; balustrade means the whole guard assembly. Replace the stair guard, upstairs landing guard and wall-mounted handrail as one coordinated package. Keep the existing stair structure and stone treads.

3. What I would do with the doors

Start by retaining sound doors and frames. Adjust sticking hinges/latches, repair chips, prepare the existing finish and repaint the leaf, frame and architrave together in a durable satin system suitable for the substrate. Make a sample first. Jotun offers a primer specifically for wood and trims; the contractor should use its compatible specified finish system. Jotun UAE product guidance.

  • Nordic: warm ivory flush doors and bronze lever handles. This is my first choice for the narrow upstairs halls.
  • Soft Classic: greige doors and brass handles. A single slim applied panel moulding can add detail where the leaf's condition and construction allow it.
  • Walnut: replace selected bedroom/office leaves with flush walnut-veneer doors, retaining compatible sound frames; use mushroom-painted secondary doors. It is the more expensive option.
  • Front door: repair/refinish the existing timber finish where its veneer permits, coordinate hardware and check smart-lock compatibility. Keep the existing exterior opening and weather seals.
  • Master bathroom: investigate a surface sliding door first. A pocket door needs a wall/services check and should not be assumed to fit. Preserve any required door rating and ventilation when modifying or replacing a leaf.

Check the floor build-up before final door trimming. The pictures show finishes, not joinery tolerances or a door schedule.

4. A larger desk with usable space

The new office boards use a target desk size of 180 × 80 cm in your approximately 3.75 × 3.55 m room. Keep it on the current desk wall, with two monitor arms, the laptop, a clear writing area and an under-desk cable tray. Reuse the black ergonomic chair and screens; add only one slim drawer pedestal so knee space stays open.

Before ordering, tape out the full footprint and chair position. Check the balcony approach, curtain movement, wardrobe-door swings and the route behind the chair. A 160 × 80 cm desk is the fallback if the measured usable wall or circulation proves tighter. A sit-stand base is included as an option within the desk allowance; the renders show it at seated height.
