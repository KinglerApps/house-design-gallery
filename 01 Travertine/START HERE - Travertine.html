Materials & design decisions

Quiet contemporary travertine: ivory and warm cream, pale natural oak veneer, subtle linear travertine texture, sand linen, restrained brushed bronze. Indoor floors are matte cream TRAVERTINE-LOOK PORCELAIN; limited real stone accents on small tables only. Clean rectangular architecture, rounded furniture, no ornamental arches, no terracotta.

These original schemes keep their original desk and stair treatment. Designs 06 onwards show the later herringbone floor, replacement rails, door treatments and larger desk.
