About this model
A design study for your house

The model follows the existing brochure geometry and measured room labels. Some shapes and heights are approximate. It is not a construction model or an exact reconstruction of the mockup photographs.

The three styles change floors, furniture, wall decoration, cabinetry and rails. The garden uses the confirmed 7.9 × 6.5 m footprint, with the terrace separate.

Walk with WASD or the thumb control. Drag the scene to look around. Use the room menu to jump between spaces. The full staircase connects both floors.
