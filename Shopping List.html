Materials affect the choice

The Homekode options use microconcrete over a wood-based core. Goploania and Aspen use sintered-stone tops; Sable uses ceramic. These have different construction despite a similar stone appearance.

Round tables need a layout check

Options 17–20 suit the garden’s materials and colours, but their round shape requires checking the chair arrangement within the pergola. A matching tabletop size alone does not confirm seating clearance.

* Sorento size and price

AED 2,730 is the starting price for the Ø 90 cm version. The Ø 120 and Ø 150 cm options require a separate price check.

Custom size and ordering

Adams Joanna is listed at AED 7,200; a 180 × 90 cm version and its price need confirmation. The Dapple link opens The Bowery Company’s collection listing. Prices throughout exclude chairs.
