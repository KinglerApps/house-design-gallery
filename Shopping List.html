Material and finish

Microcement and microconcrete are surface systems, not automatically solid cast concrete. Portia uses a plywood core. Travertin Geo and the Varaschin travertine finish are ceramics with a stone appearance; they are not natural travertine. T-Table offers natural lava stone. Order the outdoor configuration shown in the notes.

Fit, ordering and delivery

Only Portia is listed here at the exact planned 180 × 90 cm footprint. The three Adams options need custom quotes. Compact tables seat fewer people; larger tables may need the dining zone rearranged. Confirm the selected finish, chair clearance, delivery and total before ordering. Manufacturer enquiries do not imply UAE stock.
