Designs 09–11 · AED · 3 October 2026

Luxury design cost comparison.

Itemized planning ranges for the entire house, using the existing 20-area cost model and explicit increments for the new finishes. New amounts are design allowances, not contractor or supplier quotations.

The garden is 7.9 × 6.5 m. Each new garden budget replaces the previous garden scope in full. Roman and framed-wall options have no pool; Contemporary Grand Hotel includes the small plunge-pool option. The model keeps 110 m² assumed dry flooring, the larger desk and replacement indoor stair/landing rails. No structural extension or major AC replacement is included.
