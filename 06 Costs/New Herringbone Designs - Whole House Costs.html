Scope and sources

Default scope: new kitchen in the existing footprint; master shower and sliding-door allowance; full family bathroom; all-room finishes and furnishing allowances; outdoor entertaining without a pool; retained stone steps. Shared paint and flooring are counted once. Wet rooms remain tiled. Existing office chair/screens are retained; a larger desk, arms and pedestal are included. Contingency is applied once at 15%, followed by a conservative 5% VAT budget allowance.

New rail, door, subfloor and desk-accessory rates marked A-new are project allowances, not published contractor quotes. Rail rates include replacement and fixing to sound existing structure; major structural repairs are excluded. Door count excludes the master-bath slider and front door to avoid duplication. Full front-door replacement, new glazing, structural extensions and additional major AC/MEP replacement are excluded.
