What the complete scope means.

Included and counted once

  • Shared dry-area floor removal, new flooring, transitions, ordinary internal paint, external house-wall painting, mobilisation, cleaning and professional documentation / fee allowances.
  • All 20 house areas have a defined scope. The old laundry cupboard becomes storage; the former maid bathroom becomes a laundry and aquarium-maintenance room. One washer/dryer set is included there.
  • Guest-room furnishings, foyer storage, upper-hall linen cupboard repairs and the master terrace are now included.
  • Furniture allowances cover the principal items shown or described. Existing office desk, chair and monitors, aquarium tank, house glazing, entry door, carport roof and stair stone are retained.
  • Garden costs include outdoor dining, shade, lighting, planting and the particular water / pool / sunken-seating option. Pool options include plant, temperature control, a safety allowance and separate outdoor design fees.

Assumptions to resolve

  • Whole-house renovation here means a coordinated refurbishment of all areas. It does not assume demolition and replacement of every existing system or structural element.
  • Routine AC servicing is included. Major AC, rewiring, plumbing replacement or panel upgrades need inspection; the optional reserve is not a measured scope.
  • Roof replacement, major waterproofing defects, full glazing replacement, a new staircase, new facade cladding and all-new internal doors are excluded. Door / floor transitions include local making good, not wholesale door replacement.
  • Temporary accommodation, moving, off-site storage, refundable deposits, financing and future maintenance / utility bills are excluded.
  • Actual permits, consultant needs, developer charges and structural feasibility must be confirmed. Fee figures are provisional allowances, not official tariffs or permission to build.
