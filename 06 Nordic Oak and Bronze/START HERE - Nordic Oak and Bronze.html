Materials & design decisions
Floor
pale natural oak-look PVC/LVT, matte, small rectangular herringbone blocks with 90-degree interlocking ends, NOT chevron. Dry interiors only; retain wet-room tiles and stone stair treads.
Stair rails
replace all old white spindles and timber rails with slender closely spaced dark-bronze vertical metal balusters and a continuous smooth pale-oak handrail; matching pale-oak wall-mounted handrail; same system around upstairs stair opening.
Doors
retain and refinish existing flush interior doors and frames in warm ivory satin, simple bronze lever handles and matching hinges; refinish main entrance timber in pale natural oak tone without changing its construction.
Office
Larger desk, approximately 180 × 80 cm, with the existing chair and screens. Confirm clearances on site.
