Materials & design decisions

Warm Mediterranean terracotta: chalk white walls with subtle plaster texture, honey oak, earthy burnt terracotta accessories and upholstered accents, small handmade terracotta-look backsplash, aged brass and woven textures. Dry floors are warm beige stone-look PORCELAIN. Restrained rustic warmth, simple small curved decorative mirror or applied arch feature, preserve rectangular doors/windows, no new structural arches.

These original schemes keep their original desk and stair treatment. Designs 06 onwards explore new floors, replacement rails, door treatments and a larger desk.

The white garden option lightens the boundary walls and seating with white plaster, while keeping terracotta paving. This variation has not been priced separately.
